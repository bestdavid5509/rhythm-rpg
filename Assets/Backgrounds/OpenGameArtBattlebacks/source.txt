Files: Cave, DaytimeForest, DeepForest, Desert, DesertTown,
       Dungeon, EnemyFort, GoldenForest, RedForest, WinterForest
Native resolution: 1104×621 per image (painted art, not pixel art).
Project usage: scaled uniformly ~2.01× to fill 2208×1248 stage extent
       (vertical fits exactly; horizontal overflows ~5px each side
       into the camera reserve). Sprite2D texture_filter = LINEAR
       (smooths painted upscale; contrasts with the NEAREST filter
       used for pixel-art layers).
Current default for UseBackground=true: Dungeon.png
       (other 9 textures sit in this folder for future per-encounter
       background swaps; not currently referenced by code).
